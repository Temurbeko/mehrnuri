Prepared 2026-11-16. Contacts below are official public channels found by web search; verify each on the organisation's own site before sending. Anything in [square brackets] is a blank for you to fill with a real number or name. Do not send a message with a blank or a guessed number in it.

## 1. My take in one page

**Your two ideas are right in direction and wrong in order.**

- Asking Click / Payme / Uzum for *money* as an unknown solo developer with a test site will mostly get silence. They do not fund strangers.
- Asking them for something that costs them *nothing* and earns them something is realistic: every donation on MehrNuri is a payment made inside their app. You bring them transactions and a clean CSR story. That is the pitch.
- Government is slow, but one letter of support from the social protection agency is worth more than any ad: it answers "is this a scam?" for everyone else.
- You skipped the two partners with the strongest reason to say yes: **the utilities themselves** (you help them collect debts they otherwise write off) and **IT Park / StartupBase** (the real "funding without ads" door).

**Who wants what:**

| Partner | What they gain | What to ask for (small first) | Odds | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IT Park / StartupBase | A social-impact startup in their portfolio | Residency, Digital Startups Program, grant, mentor intro to fintechs | Good | 2–4 weeks |
| Utilities (HET, Hududgaz, Veolia, suv ta'minoti) | Household debts get paid by third parties | A letter of support; a way to confirm an account's debt; mention in their Telegram channel | Medium | Slow |
| Payme | Transactions + CSR + PR | Service IDs for a prefilled payment link; later a story/banner in the app | Medium | 2–6 weeks |
| Click | Same | Same; their partnerships page accepts proposals by email | Medium | 2–6 weeks |
| Uzum (Bank / holding) | Same; they already do social projects (UNDP, Taskin hospice) | Same; position as a CSR project with measurable results | Medium | Slow |
| Paynet | Same | Same | Low–medium | Slow |
| Social Protection Agency ("Inson" centres, 1140) | A free channel that helps the families they already register | Letter of support; pilot in one district where social workers tell families about MehrNuri | Low–medium | Very slow |
| Ministry of Energy | Fewer disconnections in winter | Mention / support letter | Low | Very slow |
| UNDP / UNICEF Uzbekistan | Fits poverty and energy-access programmes | Small grant or pilot | Low–medium | Months |

**The rivalry lever:** Click, Payme and Uzum compete. Tell each one, truthfully, that you are talking to all payment apps and the first to share its service IDs gets its button first and a "birinchi hamkor" mention. Do not lie about who has agreed.

## 2. What must be true before you write to anyone

Sending these messages today, with a test site and no results, burns the contacts. Do these first (all free):

1. **Real numbers.** At least [20] real families and [30] real paid bills with Soliq receipts. This needs the Uzbek server (Soliq only answers from inside Uzbekistan).
2. **A 60-second screen video.** Open a request → tap Click → the debt shows in the app → pay → the receipt verifies → the window lights up. This video *is* the pitch.
3. **A face and a legal form.** A page with your real name and photo. Companies sign with a legal entity, not a person: decide between an NNO (charity-style, fits grants) and an LLC (fits IT Park residency and contracts). Ask a lawyer once.
4. **One legal confirmation.** Uzbekistan changed the rules for collecting charitable donations (amendments to the NNO laws signed August 2025, in force November 2025). MehrNuri collects nothing, which should keep it outside those rules, but get that in writing from a lawyer. Every partner's legal team will ask.
5. **Your own domain** (mehrnuri.uz), not *.netlify.app.

## 7. What not to do

- **Don't ask a payment app for money in the first message.** Ask for service IDs. Money and promotion come after they see their own transaction numbers.
- **Don't state numbers you can't show.** Their analysts can see payments to those utility accounts.
- **Don't sign exclusivity.** A family must be payable from any app.
- **Don't let anyone route money through MehrNuri**, even a sponsor with good intentions. "We never touch the money" is the whole trust story and the reason the donation rules likely don't apply.
- **Don't promise the state agency data about families.** Share only totals.
